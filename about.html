Our Story

About Daisy H.R. Solution Pvt. Ltd.

Daisy H.R. Solution Pvt. Ltd. is a foreign employment and human resource solutions company based in Pabitranagar, Kathmandu, Nepal. We were established with a simple mission: to connect hardworking Nepali citizens with genuine, safe, and well-paying job opportunities in foreign countries.

As a manpower agency operating in Nepal's competitive foreign employment sector, we understand the hopes, concerns, and challenges Nepali workers face when seeking jobs abroad. That is why we have built our reputation on transparency, ethical recruitment practices, and personalized support from the first consultation to your safe arrival at your overseas workplace.
