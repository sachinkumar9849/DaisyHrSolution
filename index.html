About Us

About Daisy H.R. Solution Pvt. Ltd.

Daisy H.R. Solution Pvt. Ltd. is a foreign employment and human resource solutions company based in Pabitranagar, Kathmandu, Nepal. We were established with a simple mission: to connect hardworking Nepali citizens with genuine, safe, and well-paying job opportunities in foreign countries.

Our Mission

To empower Nepali workers with access to legitimate international job opportunities while protecting their rights, dignity, and financial interests throughout the foreign employment process.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and reliable foreign employment agencies in Nepal, known for ethical recruitment, worker welfare, and long-term partnerships with international employers.

What We Do

End-to-End Foreign Employment Services

One office handles the entire journey — from the first interview in Kathmandu to the day a worker boards the flight, and every renewal after that.

Overseas Recruitment & Placement

Sourcing, screening and selection of skilled, semi-skilled and unskilled workers against approved demand letters, with interviews arranged in Kathmandu or by video for overseas employers.

Read More

Skill Testing & Trade Verification

Practical trade tests for welders, masons, electricians, plumbers, drivers and machine operators, conducted at accredited centres so employers receive workers who can do the job on day one.

Read More

Documentation & Visa Processing

Passport assistance, embassy attestation, labour approval from the Department of Foreign Employment, visa stamping and ticketing — tracked file by file until the worker is cleared to fly.

Read More

Pre-Departure Orientation

The mandatory orientation training covering destination laws, workplace safety, contract rights, banking and remittance, plus practical language and cultural preparation for the Gulf and Malaysia.

Read More

Medical & Insurance Coordination

GAMCA/GCC-approved medical scheduling, re-medical follow-up where needed, foreign employment insurance and Welfare Fund registration completed before the labour permit is issued.

Manpower Supply for Employers

How It Works

Six Steps From Application to Departure

Most candidates complete the full process in 30 to 60 days, depending on the destination country and visa queue.

1

Consultation

Free counselling on trades, destinations, realistic salaries and total cost.

2

Documentation

Passport, citizenship, photos, experience letters and trade certificates verified.

3

Interview

Trade test and employer interview, in person in Kathmandu or by video call.

4

Visa & Approval

Medical, visa stamping, insurance and labour permit from the Department.

5

Training

Pre-departure orientation on rights, safety, remittance and daily life abroad.

6

Departure

Ticketing, airport briefing and arrival handover to the employer’s representative.
