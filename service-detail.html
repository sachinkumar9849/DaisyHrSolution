Overseas Recruitment & Placement

Sourcing elite talent across Nepal directly through local networks to match your enterprise demands.

Unlike generic agencies that rely on sub-agents, we coordinate directly through our own municipal networks and local offices. This guarantees authenticity of applicant profiles, mitigates recruitment fees, and creates a transparent process for both the candidate and employer.

What's Included:

  • Direct Sourcing & District Outreach
  • Multi-level Candidate Background Verification
  • Kathmandu Center Interviews (or Remote Video Calls)
  • Comprehensive Digital Shortlisting Profiles

Skill Testing & Trade Verification

Practical testing and skills evaluation at state-of-the-art facilities before job placement.

To ensure only qualified candidates reach the workplace, we manage trade-specific practical exams in fully equipped testing centers. Our standards adhere to international compliance, certifying skills in electrical work, plumbing, construction, heavy machinery operations, and hospitality trades.

Key Features:

  • Accredited Testing Center Collaboration
  • Specialized Testing for 20+ Trades
  • Trade Certifications Verification
  • Pre-departure Technical Refreshers

Accredited Grading System

Workers are graded (A, B, or C class) using practical standard checklists to ensure transparency and accurate salary matching.

Documentation & Visa Processing

Navigating government agencies, passport controls, and embassy clearances securely.

Our dedicated visa and documentation desk takes care of the bureaucratic complexity. From getting the pre-approval from the Department of Foreign Employment (DoFE) to translating contracts, completing embassy stampings, and arranging visa issuance, we monitor each candidate's file continuously.

Key Features:

  • DoFE Labour Approvals Coordination
  • Embassy Attestation & Visa Stamping
  • Contract Verification & Notarization
  • Real-time File Status Tracking

Zero-Delay System

We compile paperwork parallelly during the trade test/medical stage, cutting processing times down significantly.

Pre-Departure Orientation Training

Ensuring every departing worker is legally, culturally, and financially prepared.

Orientation is more than just a certificate requirement. Our intensive training programs guide candidates through local labor laws of destination countries, safety protocols, standard of living expectations, cultural norms, and safe banking practices to remit money home secure from middlemen.

Key Features:

  • Destination Culture & Laws Briefings
  • Workplace Safety & Rights Training
  • Financial Literacy & Remittance Demos
  • Pre-Departure Guidelines Booklet

Compliance & Rights

Our lessons place extreme focus on highlighting candidate labor rights and helpline coordinates in Gulf countries and Malaysia.

Medical & Insurance Coordination

Ensuring health clearance and insurance backing prior to deployment.

We coordinate GCC-approved medical screenings at GAMCA certified diagnostic clinics and ensure immediate registration for Foreign Employment Insurance and the Government Welfare Fund. This secures medical validity and ensures that workers and families are covered financially in any emergencies.

Key Features:

  • GAMCA/GCC Certified Clinic Bookings
  • Welfare Fund Registration Handling
  • Foreign Employment Insurance Setup
  • Post-Medical Follow-ups & Reports

Comprehensive Protection

Every worker processed through Daisy HR is insured before flight booking, complying fully with the government directives.
