  • Trade and experience screening before shortlisting
  • In-person or video interviews arranged within days
  • Complete verified file handed to the employer
Service 02

Skill Testing & Trade Verification

A worker who passes a practical trade test earns a higher grade and a higher salary. We arrange testing at accredited centres for welders, masons, electricians, plumbers, drivers and machine operators, and we re-test candidates whose certificates have expired.

  • Practical trade tests at accredited centres
  • Re-testing for expired or missing certificates
  • Employer-witnessed testing available on request
  • Grade recommendation submitted with the candidate file
Service 03

Documentation & Visa Processing

The documentation desk handles passports, embassy attestation, labour approval from the Department of Foreign Employment, visa stamping and ticketing. Each file is tracked by stage so a candidate can call and be told exactly where their paperwork stands.

  • Passport application and renewal assistance
  • Embassy and ministry attestation
  • Labour permit from the Department of Foreign Employment
  • Visa stamping, ticketing and travel scheduling
Service 04

Pre-Departure Orientation Training

The orientation certificate is a legal requirement, but the content matters more than the certificate. Workers leave the classroom knowing their contract rights, the labour law of their destination, safe working practice, and how to send money home without losing a percentage to informal channels.

  • Government-approved orientation curriculum
  • Destination labour law and worker rights
  • Workplace safety and emergency procedures
  • Banking, remittance and financial planning
Service 05

Medical & Insurance Coordination

We schedule GCC-approved medical examinations, follow up on re-medical cases, and complete foreign employment insurance and Welfare Fund registration — all before the labour permit is applied for, so nothing stalls the file at the last stage.

  • GAMCA / GCC-approved medical scheduling
  • Re-medical follow-up and second opinions
  • Foreign employment insurance policy issued
  • Welfare Fund contribution completed and recorded
Service 06

Manpower Supply for Employers

Overseas companies get a single point of contact for the whole mobilisation: demand letter processing, bulk shortlisting, trade testing, deployment scheduling and progress reporting — whether the order is for eight workers or eight hundred.

  • Demand letter and attestation handling
  • Bulk shortlisting with verified documentation
  • Batch trade testing and interview scheduling
  • Mobilisation timeline and deployment reporting
