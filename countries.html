At a Glance

Compare Destinations

Salaries vary by trade, grade and employer, so we quote them per vacancy rather than as a country average. Ask a counsellor what your trade currently earns.

Comparison of destination countries, main cities, industries, currency and typical contract length
Country Main Locations Industries We Recruit For Currency Typical Contract Notes
Qatar Doha · Al Wakrah · Al Khor Construction, Facility Management, Hospitality, Security QAR 2 years, renewable Employer-provided accommodation and food allowance are standard on most orders.
Saudi Arabia Riyadh · Jeddah · Dammam Construction, Oil & Gas Support, Transport, Retail SAR 2 years, renewable Largest volume of orders; strong demand for drivers and heavy equipment operators.
United Arab Emirates Dubai · Abu Dhabi · Sharjah Hospitality, Retail, Construction, Housekeeping AED 2 years, renewable Preferred destination for hospitality and retail roles, including female applicants.
Kuwait Kuwait City · Al Ahmadi Cleaning & FM, Catering, Technical Trades, Domestic Support KWD 2 years, renewable Facility management and catering contracts recruit in large batches.
Malaysia Kuala Lumpur · Penang · Johor Manufacturing, Electronics Assembly, Plantation, Warehousing MYR 3 years, renewable Factory work with structured overtime; hostel accommodation is normally provided.
Oman Muscat · Sohar · Salalah Construction, Logistics, Hospitality, Maintenance OMR 2 years, renewable Steady demand in logistics and maintenance trades around Muscat and Sohar.
Bahrain Manama · Riffa · Muharraq Construction, Fabrication, Hospitality, Facility Services BHD 2 years, renewable Smaller market with consistent demand for certified welders and fabricators.
